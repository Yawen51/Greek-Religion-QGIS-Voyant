# Foreign Cults in Hellenistic–Roman Greece

This project uses QGIS and Voyant Tools to study the spatial distribution and textual representation of foreign cults in Greece during the Hellenistic to Roman period (c. 4th century BCE–3rd century CE).

foreign cults were more concentrated in ports and transport nodes. In textual sources, foreign deities are often described through ritual language and cross-cultural comparison, giving them a more distinct cultural identity. By combining geographic information systems with digital text analysis, the project suggests that religious integration in this period was not a rapid process of assimilation. Instead, foreign cults were incorporated into Greek urban networks but continued to retain a degree of separation.


## Tools
- QGIS
- Voyant Tools

## Data
